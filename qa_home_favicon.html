FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to every question parents & students ask.

What NEET score do I need to get into MBBS abroad?+

For MBBS abroad (Russia, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Vietnam, Europe, Georgia etc.), you need a minimum NEET score of 150-160 for the General category, per current NMC guidelines. Individual universities may set higher thresholds. Students scoring 140–550 can have the widest range of MBBS options abroad.

Is NEET necessary for MBBS?+

MBBS admissions depend on the NEET UG cutoff, released after the entrance exam. Students must meet the cutoff and eligibility criteria. To pursue MBBS in India, aspirants must qualify for the NEET exam. Higher NEET scores increase the chances of securing a spot in prestigious medical colleges.

What are the eligibility criteria for NEET?+

An aspirant should have passed the 12th class with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) with 50% aggregate (40% for SC/ST/OBC).

What are the MBBS Fees in India?+

The MBBS course fees can be 25,000 to 1.00 lakhs. The course fee can vary based on the government and private medical colleges in India. For instance, the Index Medical College in India charges around 43,80,000, and the CM Medical College, Kuchandur, charges around 28,00,000. Government fees are much lower than those of private medical colleges, selection depends on ranks marks domicile to get cheapest pvt college or prestigious govt college.

What are the career options after MBBS?+

After completion of your MBBS Degree from a medical college, students can have multiple career opportunities, such as doctors, Resident medical officers, Medical writer, Researcher, Lecturer, etc Most students apply for MBBS abroad, but after getting an MBBS degree from abroad, students have to pass the FMGE exam to practice in India.

Which is the best country to study MBBS abroad for Indian students?+

MBBS from Russia, MBBS from U.K, MBBS from Vietnam, MBBS from Nepal, MBBS from Georgia, MBBS from Kyrgyzstan, and MBBS from Europe can be excellent choices for Medical education with affordable fees.

Which is the country with the lowest cost of studying MBBS?+

MBBS in Kyrgyzstan, MBBS in Uzbekistan, MBBS in Russia, MBBS in Kazakhstan, MBBS in Georgia and MBBS in Nepal have the lowest cost of studying, with nepal at 60 lakhs, while other abroad destination tution fee can range from 15 to 20 lakhs for the entire course duration.

What is the FMGE test?+

Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) is the medical licensing exam of India conducted by the National Medical Commission for Students who have completed their MBBS degree from abroad, so that they can do clinical practice back in India.

What are the top Universities to pursue MBBS abroad in 2026?+

The top universities to study MBBS abroad are Perm State Medical University (Russia), Orenburg State Medical University (Russia), Mari State University (Russia), Avicenna International Medical University (Kyrgyzstan), Kazan State Medical University (KSMU), Tver State Medical University (TSMU), (Russia).

Is it better to study MBBS in India or abroad?+

MBBS abroad can be a better option for Indian students than in India, as there are limited seats available in Indian medical colleges, and the level of competition is also intense in India. Medical universities abroad provide every opportunity for Indian students to receive the best medical education at low MBBS fees.

Why study MBBS abroad?+

Excellent medical education, extensive practical exposure, top clinical experience, qualified faculty, modern classrooms and labs, English-medium MBBS, comfortable accommodation and food, and affordable fees make studying MBBS abroad highly attractive.
